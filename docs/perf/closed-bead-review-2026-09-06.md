## Interpretation and exclusions

- A one-row isolated eval at the dispatch floor cannot settle the value of removing kernels inside a whole forward. Router token rows must also be distinguished from routed token-expert pairs.
- CPU graph construction before eval is not all host work. The existing wall-clock split does not measure CPU backend encoding separately from GPU execution.
- The negative scalar fused-MoE prototype is not a test of a vectorized or tiled implementation. Those alternatives already belong to `qex`. this review does not duplicate that work.
- Blanket/coarse mixed q4 policies do not test keeping all sensitive families at q8 and changing only one smaller family. Likewise, similarity among the first 128 experts at three layers does not establish an all-layer functional-merging result.
- `distinctPages` means address coverage, not observed page faults. A record can span two pages. the existing supposedly independent test repeats the starting-page assumption.
- Cached CPU GEMM is not filed from the early dequantization note: later Accelerate/SME concurrency experiments in `docs/compute-engine-map.md` already tested the broader CPU route and lost.
- `duw` says Arm B was blocked, but `.local/agreement-results.md` records the later completed Arm B plus a zeroed-head engagement control. No download retry is needed.
- The current ANE bank loss was later traced to GPU placement of per-row LUT programs. `8xv` already owns the plan-checked grouped-LUT retry; `7yp` owns loaded-GPU measurement.
- Long-context, repeated restore, and held-out quality follow-ups are evidence gaps, not claims that existing outputs are wrong. Tiny short-context agreement cannot prove those regimes.
- No source/runtime behavior was changed, no model runs were started, and existing working-tree edits were preserved.
